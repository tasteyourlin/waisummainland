About the Ingredient

Maca from the Andes

Maca is a plant in the mustard family, primarily grown at high elevations in the Andes of South America. Local residents have long used it as part of their everyday diet. Its root contains protein, amino acids, and minerals such as zinc, calcium, and iron.

This product uses maca root extract, concentrated after low-temperature gelatinization removes some of the starch.

This product is an ordinary food and is not a substitute for medication or a balanced diet. This website only explains the product ingredients, formulation, and directions for consumption; it makes no claims about effects.
