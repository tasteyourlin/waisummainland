FAQ

The answers below cover factual questions about ingredients, how to drink it, storage, and purchasing only. For any assessment related to your health, consult a physician or clinical nutritionist.

Is this a health food product?

No. This is a general food product (a beverage). It has not been registered or filed as a health food, so it makes no health-function claims and cannot replace medication.

How much should I drink each day?

Drink 1 stick daily. Tear it open and drink directly; no water is needed. Consume it all once opened.

When is a convenient time to drink it?

Any time of day is fine. We suggest choosing a consistent time so you can keep track of your daily dietary routine.

Who should not drink it?

Do not consume if you are allergic to any ingredient listed. Pregnant or breastfeeding women, children, and people taking medication or living with chronic conditions should consult a physician or clinical nutritionist before consumption.

Can I drink it with other foods or beverages?

This is a general food product and can generally be consumed as part of a normal diet. If you are taking medication, ask a physician or pharmacist whether you need to leave an interval between medicine and food.

Is it high in sugar?

Each serving (10 g) contains 2.3 g of sugar, 2.6 g of carbohydrates, 54 kJ (13 kcal) of energy, and 0 g of fat. If you need to control your sugar intake, assess your own needs or consult a professional.

Does it contain caffeine or alcohol?

The ingredient list contains no caffeine or alcohol. See the labeling page for the complete ingredients.

Does it need to be refrigerated?

Refrigeration is not required before opening. Store at room temperature in a cool, dry place, away from direct sunlight and high temperatures.

What does it taste like?

The formula is flavored with concentrated apple juice and a small amount of granulated sugar, giving it a sweet-and-tart fruity taste.

How can I identify an authentic product?

Purchase through the authorized domestic channels listed in the footer, and check the Chinese label, importer information, and production batch number on the package. Contact customer service to verify the batch number if you have questions.
