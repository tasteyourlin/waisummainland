ABOUT MACA

What kind of plant is maca?

This page introduces maca’s botanical background and composition as general information and makes no claims about this product’s effects.

Origin and growing environment

Maca (Lepidium meyenii) belongs to the mustard family and the Lepidium genus. It is native to the Andes of South America. Its main growing areas are around 4,000 m above sea level, with large day–night temperature differences, strong winds, and relatively poor soil. Local residents have long consumed maca root as a food ingredient.

The raw material used in this product is sourced from this region and added to the formula as maca root extract.

Approx. 4,000 m

In the same family as radishes and turnips; the root is the edible part.

Root extract

Part used

Extracted and concentrated for use in this product.

Protein and amino acids

Maca root contains plant protein and various amino acids, including essential amino acids that the human body cannot synthesize and must obtain from the diet.

Minerals

Mineral elements such as zinc, calcium, and iron may be detected in maca root; specific amounts vary by growing region, variety, and batch.
