LABEL & DIRECTIONS

Usage & Product Labeling

This page provides the product’s usage instructions, storage conditions, nutrition facts, and complete ingredient list.

How to Use

Take 1 sachet daily. Tear open the package and drink directly; no water is needed. It may be taken at any time of day according to your schedule. A consistent daily time is recommended to make it easier to track your dietary routine.

Consume immediately after opening. Storing it for multiple servings is not recommended.

Storage & Shelf Life

Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and high temperatures. Refrigeration is not required before opening.

Ingredient List

Listed in descending order by amount added:

Important Information

This product is a conventional food and is not a substitute for medication. Do not consume if you are allergic to any of the ingredients listed above.

Pregnant or breastfeeding women, children, and people taking medication or living with chronic conditions should consult a physician or clinical nutritionist before consumption.
